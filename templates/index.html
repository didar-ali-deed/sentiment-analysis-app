03 / Model card

What is under the hood?

DistilBERT fine-tuned on SST-2

This app serves a compact transformer classifier through ONNX Runtime. The pipeline tokenizes text, runs batched CPU inference, converts logits into calibrated class probabilities, and returns an enriched record for downstream analysis.

Use the confidence bands as a decision-support cue, not as a guarantee of correctness. The model is trained for English movie-review sentiment and may be less reliable on sarcasm, domain-specific language, or mixed sentiment.
